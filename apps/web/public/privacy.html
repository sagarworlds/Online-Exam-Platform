Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short

Online Exam Platform runs online exams for schools, colleges and other organisers. We use your personal data to let you sign in, take exams, see your results and, where the organiser has set it up, to notify you, including by WhatsApp. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and to withdraw your consent, at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Online Exam Platform (“we”, “us”) handles personal data when you use our website and exam service. It covers candidates who sit exams, parents or guardians linked to a candidate, and staff of an exam organiser who set up and run exams.

An exam organiser is the school, college, institute or company that invites you to an exam. The organiser decides who is invited, what the exam is and how it is run. We apply the law that applies to us, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. What we collect

Account Your name, email address and/or mobile number (used to send you sign-in codes), date of birth, and a password if you use one. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash. We also keep the role you hold, such as candidate or exam staff.
Parent or guardian details For a candidate who is a child: the guardian’s name and contact details, the link between them, and the guardian’s consent.
Exam activity The invitations you accepted; when you start and finish an exam; the answers you choose and the questions you mark for review; your score and result, and any later correction of it; and requests you make, such as asking for another attempt, with the reason you give.
Exam integrity Where the organiser turns the checks on: each time you leave the exam page or full screen, warnings an invigilator sends you, and whether an invigilator paused or ended your attempt. We do not use your camera, microphone or screen recording.
Sign-in and device Your IP address and a device signature (a short code derived from your browser and device settings) when you sign in and during an attempt, the times of your sessions, and a log of actions staff take. We use these to keep accounts secure and to notice when one account is used from two places.
Accommodations If the organiser gives you an accommodation, such as extra time, a reader or scribe, or a different display, we store what was given and an optional note written by staff. This can reveal a disability or other need, so it is shown only to the staff who manage the exam, and to you for what applies to you.
Consent records What you agreed to, which version of the notice you saw, and when you agreed or withdrew.
Messages The email address or mobile number we send notices to, the notices themselves (for example invitations, reminders and result notices) and whether they were delivered.
On your device We use your browser’s local storage, not advertising or tracking cookies, to keep you signed in and to remember your language and display choices (text size, contrast) and which questions you have visited. You can clear it in your browser settings; you will then be signed out.

3. Why we use it

  • to create your account and check it is you signing in;
  • to deliver exams, keep the time, save your answers, mark them and show you the result and review;
  • to keep exams fair and to look into suspected cheating, always with a person making the decision;
  • to give you any accommodation you are entitled to;
  • to send you notices about exams you are invited to, and to answer your requests and disputes about a result;
  • to keep the service secure, find faults and keep a record of administrator actions; and
  • to meet legal obligations.

We use your data on the basis of your consent and, where the law allows it, for the purposes above that the law recognises without consent. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it from the Consent page after you sign in; withdrawing does not undo what was done before.

4. WhatsApp and other messages

We send sign-in codes and notices by email. Where an exam organiser switches it on, and you have given your mobile number to take part in an exam and agreed to receive them, we may also send notices such as invitations, reminders and result notices by WhatsApp, using the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and its affiliates (“Meta”).

  • To send a WhatsApp message we give Meta your mobile number and the text of the message. Meta also receives delivery information.
  • Messages are limited to these notices. We do not put your exam answers in them, and we do not use WhatsApp for advertising.
  • Meta handles that data under its own terms and privacy policies: WhatsApp Privacy Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.
  • You can stop WhatsApp notices at any time by telling us (see Contact), by withdrawing your consent, or by blocking the sender in WhatsApp. You can still take your exam without them.

5. Who we share it with

  • The exam organiser that invited you: its staff can see your account details, your attempts, answers, scores, integrity events and any accommodation, because they run the exam.
  • Service providers that work for us: hosting and database providers, email delivery, and Meta for WhatsApp where it is switched on. They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it or to protect people and our service.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it with advertisers. Some providers, including Meta, may process data outside India.

6. Children

We ask for your date of birth so that we and the organiser know whether a candidate is under 18. Where the law requires the consent of a parent or guardian before a child’s personal data is processed, it must be obtained first. Parents and guardians can contact us (see Contact) about their child’s data. We do not show advertising to anyone.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as it is needed for the purposes in this policy or as the law requires, and then delete or anonymise it. Exam records, such as attempts and results, are kept as long as the organiser needs a record of the result. Security and audit logs are kept for the period the law requires. You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see Your rights); we will do so unless we must keep it by law.

8. How we protect it

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), store passwords only as hashes, limit what each role can see and do, allow one active session per candidate during an exam, and keep an audit log of administrators’ actions. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of personal data affects you, we will tell the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected, as the law requires.

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it, and to erase it when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw a consent you gave, as easily as you gave it;
  • use our grievance process below, and complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we do not resolve your complaint; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot do so yourself.

To use a right, write to us at the address below. We may need to check it is you first.

10. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in a way you would not expect, we will tell you before it applies, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

11. Contact and grievances

For anything about your exam, your result or an accommodation, contact the exam organiser that invited you.

For anything about how this platform handles your personal data, including using your rights above or making a complaint, write to us at the email address that sent you your invitation or sign-in code, with “Privacy” in the subject line. Say who you are and which exam you are connected with so that we can find your records.
